THIRTY DAYS OF dbt – NDR EdTech (Telugu)
Course outline (this file is only an index; the voice-over scripts are day1.txt to day30.txt)

Common project for the whole series: Banking project.
Source data lands in Snowflake database BANK_DB, schema RAW (tables CUSTOMER and TRANSACTIONS).
dbt builds staging models and business-ready marts in schemas DBT_DEV (development) and ANALYTICS (production).
Tools used: dbt Core with the Snowflake adapter (dbt-snowflake).
