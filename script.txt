[Explaining – Main Concept]

Snowflake Time Travel ante previous state lo unna data ni access cheyyadaniki, recover cheyyadaniki use chese feature.

Dheenitho manam accidentally deleted tables ni recover cheyyachu, previous data ni query cheyyachu, and historical changes ni compare cheyyachu.

Time Travel lo AT and BEFORE keywords use chesi specific timestamp, offset, or statement-based historical data ni access cheyyachu.
